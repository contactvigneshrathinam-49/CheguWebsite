POLITICAL DOCUMENTARY & CAMPAIGN

During the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly election campaign, I worked as a photographer and videographer in the Tirupathur constituency. The project involved documenting election campaign activities, public interactions, and critical moments from the electoral process.

Alongside the campaign coverage, I produced a documentary highlighting public service and community initiatives. Through visual storytelling, the project aimed to create an enduring record combining documentary filmmaking with political and grassroots field photography.

**KALIMAN — CHILDREN’S CREATIVE LEARNING**

I documented Kaliman's workshops conducted for children across Tamil Nadu, capturing their creative learning experiences through photography. The workshops included storytelling, clay toy-making, drawing, play, and other creative activities.

My work focused on documenting the children's imagination, participation, interactions, and creative expression throughout these sessions. Through visual storytelling, I aimed to preserve the experiences of the children and highlight the importance of creativity, play, and storytelling in children's learning and development.

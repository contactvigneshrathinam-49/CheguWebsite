CHILDREN’S WALL

For children, their home is their first canvas. In this piece, I have depicted the lives, dreams, and joys of the children who were evicted from their homes in Govindasamy Nagar, Chennai in 2022. Through my art, I hope to give viewers insight into the psychological distress these children face because of eviction.
